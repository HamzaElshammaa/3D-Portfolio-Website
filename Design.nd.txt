## Brand & Style

This design system occupies the tension between archival print luxury and hyper-contemporary digital brutalism. Designed for an avant-garde creative studio, digital gallery, or high-concept atelier, the interface treats the viewport as an exhibition space where physical magazine pacing meets kinetic interaction.

The personality is uncompromising, intellectual, and electrifying:
- **Atmospheric Contrast:** Bone-colored editorial expanses abruptly punctured by high-voltage acid lime and deep carbon ink.
- **Architectural Rigor:** Exposed grid lines, hairline structural framing, and strict modular geometry strip away standard consumer UI softness.
- **Kinetic Stature:** Colossal display numbers, dynamic baseline shifts, and theatrical typographic scales designed to support fluid scroll mechanics and 3D web graphics.
- **Tone:** Authoritative, curated, boundary-pushing, and uncompromisingly precise.

## Colors

The palette is engineered for severe graphic contrast, balancing organic parchment tones with industrial carbon and synthetic neon.

- **Neutral Canvas (`#E9F1DF`):** A pale, mineral-tinted cream functioning as the tactile substrate. It avoids sterile digital white in favor of heavyweight editorial card stock.
- **Carbon Ink (`#0F0F11`):** A saturated black with minimal warm dispersion. Used for structural grid hairlines, razor typography, and solid brutalist blocks.
- **Acid Lime (`#CCFF00`):** An electric, high-chroma accent deployed exclusively for points of kinetic friction: interactive focus triggers, status pings, and selected states. Never diluted or used for long-form reading.
- **Dark Mode Alternative:** The system reverses into `#121212` matte obsidian as the base canvas, retaining `#0F0F11` for sub-panels, `#E9F1DF` for stark hairline dividers and typography, and `#CCFF00` as the primary tactical glow.

## Typography

The typographic system fuses expressive display forms with technical grotesk geometries.

- **Headlines & Expressive Titles:** Set in **Syne**. Features heavy weights (`700`, `800`), geometric proportions, and deliberate optical friction at massive scales. Tight tracking and compressed leading produce monolithic headline locks.
- **Body & Data:** Set in **Space Grotesk**. Delivers technical clarity, disciplined tabular metrics, and raw editorial texture across paragraphs and metadata.
- **Hero Numerals:** Giant tabular numerals designed to function as kinetic anchors, project indexers, and parallax counters.
- **Labels:** Rendered exclusively in upper case with wide letter-spacing (`0.08em` to `0.12em`) to mirror archival print notation, coordinate systems, and technical metadata stamps.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is an explicit editorial broadsheet grid:

- **Grid Architecture:** 12-column rigid matrix on desktop, 6-column on tablet, and 2-column or single-column on mobile. Columns connect via hairline borders rather than empty gutter channels (`gutter: 0px`). Modules sit edge-to-edge in contiguous geometric cells.
- **Structural Margins:** Canvas margins form an architectural mat board around content (`2rem` desktop, `1rem` mobile), bordered by a continuous perimeter line.
- **Rhythm & Padding:** Internal content uses tight multiples (`space-sm` to `space-xl`) inside structural cells to maintain consistent typographic baselines. Modules stack flush against adjacent borders.
- **Responsive Adaptation:** Grid modules reflow vertically on mobile breakpoints without breaking their bounding hairline enclosures.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system rejects drop shadows, atmospheric blurs, and soft skeuomorphic gradients. Spatial separation is achieved through sharp contrast and mechanical surface transitions:

- **Hairline Outlines:** All content segmentation relies on crisp `1px` solid outlines in Carbon (`#0F0F11`) on light backgrounds or Cream (`#E9F1DF`) on dark backgrounds.
- **Flat Inversion Tiers:** Active states, hovered panels, and overlay layers invert their surfaces: background switches instantaneously from `#E9F1DF` to `#0F0F11`, and foreground text snaps to `#CCFF00` or `#E9F1DF`.
- **Z-Index Layering:** Modals, navigation drawers, and floating panels are hard-edged rectangular cutouts bordered by `2px` solid Carbon outlines, floating directly over content without soft ambient occlusion.

## Shapes

The shape system is strictly architectural and non-radiused:

- **Zero-Radius Mandate:** Every container, button, chip, input field, and interactive tile features `0px` border radius (`roundedness: 0`).
- **Right Angles:** Interfaces are treated as interlocking mechanical plates. Cutouts and structural dividers emphasize strict perpendicular axes.
- **Sharp Truncations:** Diagonal chamfers or hard linear clipping masks may be used for technical markers, but round forms and pill shapes are prohibited.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid Carbon `#0F0F11` background, `#E9F1DF` uppercase text, `0px` radius, `1px` Carbon border. On hover: background flashes instantaneously to `#CCFF00`, text snaps to `#0F0F11`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Transparent background, `1px` Carbon border, Carbon text. On hover: background fills with `#0F0F11`, text turns `#CCFF00`.
- **Kinetic Action:** Displayed with an index numeral prefix (e.g., `[ 01 ] ENTER ARCHIVE`) and right-aligned arrow glyphs.

### Chips & Badges
- Modular tags constructed with `1px` solid borders, `space-xs` vertical and `space-sm` horizontal padding.
- Text set in `label-xs` uppercase. Active or spotlighted chips fill with solid `#CCFF00` and Carbon text.

### Input Fields
- Underlined-only or flush framed boxes with `1px` solid Carbon perimeter. Transparent fill.
- Typographic label sits directly on the upper perimeter or pinned above in `label-xs` tracking.
- Focused state replaces the base border with a `2px` solid Carbon line accompanied by a subtle `#CCFF00` corner tick or active status marker.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Checkbox:** Raw `16px × 16px` square with `1px` Carbon border. Checked state fills the box with solid Carbon containing an internal `#CCFF00` square pip (no curved checkmarks).
- **Radio:** Diamond or sharp square variant with a centered dot in `#CCFF00`. Rounded radio geometries are omitted.

### Cards & Exhibition Tiles
- Bordered on all four sides by `1px` Carbon lines, tiling edge-to-edge with neighboring cards.
- Content structured into partitioned cells: metadata and catalog index pinned to top rows, primary asset/media occupying the center frame, and title locks anchored to the bottom.
- On hover, tile borders thicken to `2px` or reverse color values.

### Lists & Indexes
- Continuous broadsheet rows separated by `1px` horizontal hairline borders.
- Monospaced tabular index on the left (`001`, `002`), title in `headline-md`, and metadata tags aligned to the right grid columns.
- Hover expands the row vertically or reveals an inline thumbnail, accompanied by a foreground flash to `#CCFF00`.

### Typographic Tickers & Coordinate Bars
- Fixed viewport header and footer strips running continuous archival coordinates, timestamp clocks, and ticker streams in `label-xs`, anchoring the editorial frame.
